A catenable, splittable, transient sequence — in Racket

sek, measured

Charguéraud and Pottier's chunked sequence, ported to Racket, against Racket's own sequence types. The first group of measurements follows the benchmark suite that ships with the authors' OCaml library and the two figures in the paper. The second is transcribed from the suites that ship with the other chunked-sequence libraries — Scala's Vector, immer, and bifurcan — so that those comparisons are ones their authors chose.

A structure whose push walks the sequence cannot be grown to n one push at a time -- that loop is quadratic -- so those rows are measured as a bounded burst against a sequence already at length n.
